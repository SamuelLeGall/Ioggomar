# Evasion & Hit Rate System

## **Overview**

Not all attacks hit. The system balances **accuracy and evasion** based on stats.

## **Hit Calculation**

- **Formula**: `Hit Chance = Base Accuracy - (Target Evasion * Modifier)`.
- **Critical Hits** occur based on **luck** and **dexterity**.

## **Evasion Factors**

- **Speed vs Dexterity** – Faster units evade more.
- **Buffs/Debuffs** – Some skills increase hit rate or evasion.

To prevent abuse, **minimum and maximum hit chances** exist.
